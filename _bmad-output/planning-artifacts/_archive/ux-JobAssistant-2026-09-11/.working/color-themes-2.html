JobAssistant — Beige Light-Theme Exploration

Pure exploration, not a proposal: the dark "Violet Precision" theme is already confirmed (near-black background, off-white text, indigo-violet accent, professional/sharp/technical, explicitly not playful or warm). This page previews four distinct beige-based LIGHT themes — each with its own register, not variations on one idea — so it's easy to see whether warm beige is worth pursuing at all, or whether the confirmed dark direction should stand as-is. The confirmed dark theme is reproduced at the end, unchanged, for direct side-by-side comparison.

4 beige explorations + 1 confirmed dark theme for comparison — nothing here replaces the confirmed decision

professional and sharp, warmth kept to a minimum
