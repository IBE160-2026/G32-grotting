- (decision) Brief purpose: pitch for a course investor-meeting exercise (IBE160), not a live raise - skip financials/funding ask, keep investor-pitch shape (problem/market/differentiation/product/why-now).
- (decision) Target users: university/college students hunting for their first FULL-TIME job (not internships). No institution/career-center tie-in.
- (decision) Differentiation vs incumbents (Kickresume free 6mo, Teal free-forever): dual wedge - (1) AI-transparency feature giving users real insight into how AI/ATS/recruiters judge CVs and applications, (2) gap-analysis that translates coursework/projects/extracurriculars into legible qualifications for students with no work history.
- (decision) Business model: Free (course project, no monetization needed).
- (decision) Timeline: working web application due early December 2026 - stage is pre-build/spec, framed honestly as pre-launch, no fabricated traction.
- (event) Web research digest on AI CV/job-app tool landscape gathered (Kickresume, Rezi, Teal, Jobscan, Enhancv comparables; pricing norms 4-50/mo freemium; student no-work-history gap identified as whitespace; human reviewers penalize generic AI text).
- (decision) Problem section finalized: catch-22 framing (ATS keyword-matching structurally favors work-experience vocabulary students don't have), no fabricated stats, kept qualitative.
- (decision) Solution section finalized: upload-CV-once + paste-job-ad flow, learns voice from past applications, ATS-style match score with keyword-level explanations (AI-transparency feature), gap analysis suggests coursework/project-based fixes rather than just flagging gaps.
- (decision) Differentiation section finalized: honest that there is no technical moat; the real differentiator is the transparency-first product philosophy (show why, not just beat the filter) vs incumbents' black-box 'beat the filter' approach.
- (decision) Who This Serves finalized: primary user is any-field university/college student applying for first full-time job; purely self-serve/B2C, no institutional intermediary.
- (decision) Success Criteria finalized: user success = suggestions feel honest/useful and defensible in interviews; delivery success = working end-to-end demo by early Dec, grading criteria out of scope for brief.
- (decision) Scope finalized: v1 = CV upload, job ad paste-in, tailored letter, CV suggestions, gap analysis w/ coursework translation, ATS match score w/ explanations, login + encrypted storage. Out of v1: voice-learning from past applications, mobile app, multi-language, auto-apply, interview prep, LinkedIn import.
