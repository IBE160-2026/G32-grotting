JobAssistant — Brown/Mocha-Accent Tone Pick

Warm Editorial's beige/cream base (sand background, warm brown text) and its success/error semantics are unchanged from color-themes-2.html and color-themes-3.html. Instead of the pink accent explored previously, the accent family here is brown/mocha, shown in 2 distinct registers — a lighter, softer caramel and a deeper, richer mocha — so the exact final tone can be picked directly. The warning color has been re-tuned to a vivid goldenrod so it can never be mistaken for a third brown.
